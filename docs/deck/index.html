The business challenge
Acme Retail's paid traffic lands on a generic store
A first-time visitor and a loyal repeat buyer see the same homepage, rails, and offers.
  • Low session-to-purchase conversion and high bounce on landing pages
  • About 30% of sessions are cold start, with no history to personalize on
  • Merchandisers can't see why a product was shown, or whether it worked
  • Personalization logic lives in app code, away from the analytics the business trusts

Section 02
The Databricks solution, from click to recommendation
The solution
Three capabilities on one governed platform
Real-time signal
Lakeflow streams clickstream into governed tables. Lakebase serves features and live session state in milliseconds.
Intelligence
A two-stage recommender on Model Serving, Vector Search for cold start, and grounded LLM explanations.
Business loop
An A/B experiment, a business case in dollars, a Genie space, and a merchandiser copilot.
Architecture
Every stage consumes the previous stage's output
How it works
Four steps turn a click into the next recommendation
1
Capture the click
The app writes each view, add-to-cart, and purchase to Lakebase session state.
2
Look up context
User features and the last 5 clicks come back from Lakebase in about 5 ms.
3
Rank candidates
ALS and embedding candidates are ranked by LightGBM on Model Serving.
4
Explain and log
Claude writes a grounded "why this?" and every impression is logged for attribution.
Built for production
Governed, resilient, and explainable by design
01
Governed end to end
Email masked and tagged as PII, regional row filters, and lineage from landing volume to served model in Unity Catalog.
02
Resilient serving
If Model Serving is slow, precomputed recommendations in Lakebase fill the rail, so shoppers never see an empty page.
03
Explainable and measured
Champion and challenger models in Unity Catalog, an A/B readout, and a copilot scored 20 of 20 on grounded answers.
